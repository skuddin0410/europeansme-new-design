How we keep these tools accurate

1 · A named owner

Every tool and rule page has an editor on our compliance desk who is responsible for it. Their name is on the page.

2 · Checked every month

Deadlines, rates and thresholds are re-checked monthly against EUR-Lex, the European Commission and national tax authorities. The last-checked date is shown on every result.

3 · Guidance, not legal advice

Our tools show what is likely to apply to you and link to the official source. Confirm with an adviser before you act on them.
